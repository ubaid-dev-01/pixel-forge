# PixelForge brand

Source: PixelForge Brand Identity Guide.

## Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Navy | `#0C1127` | Primary text, dark surfaces |
| Deep Blue | `#011F55` | Logo depth, pressed states |
| Teal Blue | `#1E82A2` | Primary accent / CTAs |
| Light Accent | `#6EC1E4` | Highlights, favicon pixels |

## Typography

- Headings / logo: **Sora** (600)
- Body / UI: **Inter** (400 / 500 / 600)

## Logo

Faceted geometric **P** with a three-pixel cluster at the top-left. Use `Logo` component or `/brand/logo*.svg`.

## Tagline

Professional image infrastructure for people who care about visual quality.
